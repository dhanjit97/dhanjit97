## About

I'm focused on building practical software and understanding how things work under the hood. My interests span **software engineering, artificial intelligence, computer vision, application development, and systems**.

I enjoy working across different layers of a project—from designing an interface and connecting a database to experimenting with machine-learning models and exploring lower-level systems.

I prefer learning by building, testing ideas, and improving them through implementation.

## Current Focus

Alongside my public repositories, I'm working through ideas and experiments in a few areas:

- **Computer-vision attendance:** Exploring face recognition from classroom or group photos and date-based attendance tracking.
- **Desktop applications:** Developing my approach to C++/Qt applications for admission and institutional workflows.
- **CommonCart:** Reworking the design and architecture of a multi-vendor marketplace, with an emphasis on a manageable foundation for future development.
- **Systems programming:** Exploring Rust, Linux, and peer-to-peer networking concepts.

These are works in progress, not claims of completed or production-ready releases.
